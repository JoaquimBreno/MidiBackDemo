MIDIBack

Automatic pitch correction conditioned on symbolic accompaniment. Vocal and backing notes share one OctupleMIDI sequence; an Adversarial-MidiBERT encoder predicts corrected pitch tokens.

Listening clips use Praat TD-PSOLA via Parselmouth to apply predicted / corrupted pitch shifts to the vocal waveform. Backing window transposition for the case study uses pyrubberband.

Architecture

Cascaded pipeline: GAME / NS-SPP vocal transcription and MuScriptor backing transcription feed a single CNPP sequence; corrected MIDI pitches drive APC resynthesis offline.

Figure from the accompanying paper draft MIDIBack: Harmony-Aware Automatic Pitch Correction Conditioned on Symbolic Accompaniment.
